Privacy Policy

Applies to the RepIo app for Android (package com.grafizum.repio). Last updated 5 November 2026.

RepIo is a workout planner and tracker. It is built so that your training data stays on your own phone, and so that the app sends us nothing at all. There is no account, no subscription, no advertising and no analytics profile.

1. Who is responsible

RepIo is published by Grafizum, a sole proprietorship established in Switzerland. Grafizum is the controller for the processing described below. Operator details are in the Legal Notice.

For any question, request or complaint about this policy, write to privacy@grafizum.com or, if you prefer the address used in earlier versions of this policy, grafizum@gmail.com.

2. What stays on your device

Everything you create in RepIo is stored locally on your phone, in the app's private storage. That includes:

  • your training plan and weekly schedule;
  • the exercises you log, with sets, reps, weight and dates;
  • your levels, progression and the history behind them;
  • your settings, including the theme you choose and your equipment selection.

This data is not sent to us or to anyone else. It is not backed up to our servers, and we cannot read it. Removing it is entirely in your control: uninstalling the app deletes it, and clearing the app's storage in Android settings does the same.

RepIo has no user account. We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, date of birth, location, contacts or photos, and we do not create a profile about you.

3. What leaves your phone: nothing

RepIo has no server of its own and no account system. It does not transmit your training data, your settings or your usage anywhere.

Nothing is sent by the app

The only network request RepIo makes is to Google Play, to ask whether a newer version exists when you open it. That request is handled by Google under Google's privacy policy and carries no workout data. RepIo has no support form that uploads anything, no database of user messages, and no analytics or advertising SDK.

If you choose to email us

The support button in Settings opens your own email app with a message already addressed to repio@grafizum.com and the app version in the subject. RepIo itself sends nothing: there is no message until you press send in that app, and you can edit or discard it first. If you do write to us, we receive your email address, what you write, and whatever technical information your mail provider attaches to an email, such as the time it was sent. We use it only to answer you.

Earlier versions of RepIo had an in-app support form that stored messages in a Google Firebase database, protected by Firebase App Check. That path has been removed from the app, together with the Firebase libraries it used. No server receives anything from RepIo any more.

4. What we do not do

  • No collection at all: the app has no server and transmits nothing.
  • No advertising, and no advertising identifier.
  • No analytics or usage tracking, and no third-party tracking SDKs.
  • No crash reporting service; if the app crashes, nothing is sent to us.
  • No sale or sharing of personal data with anyone.
  • No access to your location, camera, microphone, contacts, calendar, SMS, photos or files.
  • No connection to Google Fit, Health Connect or any other health platform.

5. Why we process it, and on what basis

We process an email you send us only in order to answer it. Under the Swiss FADP we rely on processing in good faith, purpose limitation and proportionality. Where the GDPR applies, the basis is our legitimate interest in answering the people who write to us, and, where you are asking us to do something for you, the steps needed to respond to that request.

6. How long we keep it

Emails you send us are kept until your request is resolved, and then for no longer than 12 months so that we can recognise a recurring problem. After that they are deleted. Data on your device is kept for as long as you keep the app installed, and is deleted when you uninstall it. We never hold a server-side copy of your training data, because none is ever sent to us.

7. Who else is involved

Google Play distributes the app and handles the update check, under Google's own terms. Our email is handled by our mail provider. We do not transfer personal data to anyone else, except where the law requires it.

Because these providers operate internationally, an email you send us may be stored outside Switzerland or the European Economic Area, under the safeguards those providers have in place.

8. Your rights

You can ask us for a copy of the emails you sent us, ask us to correct them, or ask us to delete them. You can also object to our processing or ask us to restrict it. Write to privacy@grafizum.com and we will respond within 30 days.

You also have the right to complain to a supervisory authority: in Switzerland, the Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner (FDPIC); in the EU or EEA, the authority in your country of residence.

9. Children

RepIo is intended for adults and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect data from children. Because the app has no account and stores training data locally, a child using it does not create a record with us.

10. Health and fitness disclaimer

RepIo provides general fitness and training information. It is not a medical device and does not provide medical advice, diagnosis or treatment. If you have a medical condition, are pregnant, or are recovering from injury, speak to a qualified healthcare professional before starting or changing a training programme.

11. Security

Data on your device is protected by Android's app sandbox. Because the app transmits nothing, there is no server-side store of your training data that could be breached. An email you choose to send is protected in transit by your mail provider and by ours. No system is perfectly secure, but we hold as little as it is possible to hold.

12. Changes to this policy

If the app's data behaviour changes, this page is updated before the change ships, and the date at the top changes with it. Material changes will also be described in the app's release notes.
