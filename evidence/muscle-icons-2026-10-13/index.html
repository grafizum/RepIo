Muscle group glyphs — traced from the drawings

Each card: the owner's drawing, the glyph that was in the app, and the traced glyph. The drawing column is the source PNG; the other two are read straight out of composeApp/src/main/res/drawable/ic_muscle_*.xml, so they are the same path data the app renders, tinted with the theme primary. The big one is 88px so the line can be judged; the small one next to it is the real 22dp of the filter row, and the row underneath is how it reads collapsed. The kit glyphs at the bottom are the style authority: stroke only, round caps, 1.6, one 24dp box.
