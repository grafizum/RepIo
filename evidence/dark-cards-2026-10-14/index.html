The dark ladder is the beige theme's steps, mirrored

A dark palette is not picked by eye, it is derived from the light one: measure each of beige's steps away from its canvas, then choose the grey that reproduces that ratio above the dark canvas. Its canvas is the theme's lightest tone, the dark theme's is its darkest, so the same picture is the same sizes in the other direction.

One pair is deliberately inverted, and it is the pair the owner named: beige's hero card (1.107) is a smaller step than its bar (1.157), so a strict mirror would put the dark card below the dark bar. He asked for the opposite — bar and general cards down, the changed card up — and he is looking at a screen rather than at a table: a card is a panel and the bar is chrome, and the panel has to come out in front. So the bar took a rung of its own at #1B1B1B (1.121:1) and the hero card kept 1.158:1, which is the only way the card could be lighter than the bar without leaving the whisper band the palette allows (1.2:1).

The nested well is the largest departure of all, and it is the one the owner caught. Beige's well is 1.297:1 from its canvas — the widest step that theme takes — so a strict mirror puts the dark well at #282828, lighter than the hero card, i.e. a raised highlight rather than the recessed block it reads as in beige. The first fix went the other way and landed it exactly on the canvas (#0E0E0E, 1.000:1) — mirror-correct by ratio to the CARD, invisible against the PAGE, and the rep banner read as a hole punched through the card. It now takes the ladder's card rung (#191919, 1.098:1): the same grey every other card in the app wears, one step under the hero card, and visible in its own right. The rule this bought is worth keeping: a step that only exists relative to the card has to stay visible against the page as well, and the test now asserts both.

The proof is that the two appearances now carry the same colour shares: on Home the bar is 9.3% at #2C2C2C → 9.2% at #1B1B1B against beige's 9.2%, and the general cards 3.1% against beige's 3.1%.
