Muscle group icons

The six glyphs behind the muscle filter's collapsed row, read straight out of composeApp/src/main/res/drawable/ic_muscle_*.xml - the same path data the app renders, tinted with the THEME PRIMARY exactly like the equipment glyphs (one ink on both surfaces, the same tint [equipmentGlyphTint] returns in the app). Each is ONE body part as a muscle outline, in the reference arm's own style: an arm, one leg, one shoulder, one pec, one lat, the abs. The strip at the top is the equipment set they were redrawn to match. Left column is a light surface (Warm Beige), right column the dark theme. The small one is the real 22dp size; the large one is there so you can judge the drawing itself. The row under each card is how it will read in the filter.
