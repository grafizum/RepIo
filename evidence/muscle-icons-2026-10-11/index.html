Muscle group icons

The six glyphs behind the muscle filter's collapsed row, read straight out of composeApp/src/main/res/drawable/ic_muscle_*.xml - the same path data the app renders, tinted with the app's own per-group ink. Each is ONE body part drawn alone: an arm, a pair of legs, a chest, a back, a core, a pair of shoulders. The strip at the top is the equipment set they were redrawn to match. Left column is a light surface (Warm Beige), right column the dark theme. The small one is the real 22dp size; the large one is there so you can judge the drawing itself. The row under each card is how it will read in the filter.
