The gap is 42px = 16.0dp (pure background) — the column's own spacedBy(16.dp) and nothing else. It used to carry an extra sectionSpacing spacer on top, which made this one gap 16 + 16 + 16 = 48dp while every other gap on the screen stayed 16dp. Both cards wear the same full-strength accent rim: the level card used its own inline tint (primary.copy(alpha = 0.35f)), which equals workoutCardRim on a light scheme but is a WASH of the accent on a dark one — so on the theme captured the two cards wore visibly different edges. Both now read the rim from the one shared function.
