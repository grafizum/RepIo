The dark themes' primary action: a tone of the accent, not the accent

Owner, 2026-10-14: "the other dark themes are too strong ... i want them to be less strong on the eye especially if the primary color is shown orange in the my workout tab it is very heavy on the eye, id prefer to keep it less strong if possible". Both captures below are the FLAME theme's My Workout, on emulator-5554, one build apart.

The vivid orange falls from 8.34% of the frame to 2.18% — a 74% cut in how much of the screen is saturated accent. What replaces it is the button's own fill, a deep tone of the same hue (#331F15, which is 1.24:1 from the canvas — a panel, not a glare), with the accent kept as the label: #FF6D00 on it, which is 5.52:1.

What is left of the accent, and why

The 2.18% that remains is the accent doing a job: the button's label and icon, the two workout cards' 1dp rims, the level number, the progress bar. Those are lines and words. The thing removed was the accent used as an AREA, which is the same rule the cards had already adopted — workoutCardFill is a tint and workoutCardRim a line — and the primary action was the last place still breaking it.

Scope

  • Toned: the dark themes that are not the developer skin — FLAME, PURPLE, OCEAN, ROSE.
  • Untouched: DEVELOPER (the hand-tuned accent gold the owner asked for) and the four LIGHT themes (BEIGE, EMERALD, CRIMSON, ROYAL_BLUE), whose accents never glared.
  • Every solid primary button on My Workout goes through one function (workoutActionButtonColors()) — the save-set button, Restart, Start workout again, previous/next, and the demo buttons — because ten independent call sites are exactly how the slab got there.

Held by three cases in DarkThemeContrastTest: the tone must not be the raw accent and must be quieter than it, the label must clear 4.5:1 on the tone in every dark theme, and the developer skin plus the light themes must keep their solid fill. Reverting the tone to the raw accent fails two of them.
